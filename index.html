The Kitchen

Built during a pandemic. Built to last past it.

D6 Kitchens started from a simple bet: that a single, well-run kitchen could responsibly carry more than one restaurant's worth of ambition — if the sourcing, the recipes, and the discipline behind the pass were taken seriously.

It launched into a moment when dining rooms had closed and delivery had become the only door left open. Rather than run one brand thinly, we built eight — each with its own cuisine, its own recipes, and its own reason to exist — sharing one supply chain, one hygiene standard, and one kitchen floor.

The result reads, to a customer, like eight different restaurants. Behind the pass, it runs like one disciplined operation.

Sourcing & Standards

Eight menus. One standard.

AROMATICS ON HAND

Lemongrass and kaffir lime leaf are kept fresh in the same pantry as everything else — the backbone of Samsara's curries and broths, and a small sign that no single brand's ingredients are treated as an afterthought.

01 — INGREDIENTS

Sourced fresh, daily

Produce, protein, and dairy are ordered against next-day covers, not stockpiled — every brand draws from the same fresh-sourcing discipline, regardless of ticket size.

02 — RECIPES

Built by cuisine, not by shortcut

Each brand's menu was developed around its own cuisine's real technique — a Thai gravy is built like a Thai gravy, not adapted from whatever base sauce is on hand.

03 — KITCHEN FLOOR

One hygiene standard, no exceptions

Every brand shares the same production floor, the same food-safety protocol, and the same quality checks at the pass — there's no "back kitchen" version of the rules.

04 — PEOPLE

Chefs who own a cuisine

Each menu is run by a section that specialises in that cuisine — the Tibetan Affair section doesn't touch the Yalla Kebab grill, and vice versa.

How It Works

From one order to eight restaurants' worth of choice.

STEP 1

Order arrives

A customer orders from any of the eight brands, on any delivery app, from any address in range.

STEP 2

Ticket is routed

The order prints to the correct brand section of the kitchen — its own recipes, its own line.

STEP 3

Fired & plated

Cooked to that brand's standard, packed under that brand's identity — never a shared shortcut.

STEP 4

Out the door

Picked up and delivered as if it came from a dedicated restaurant — because, in every way that matters, it did.
